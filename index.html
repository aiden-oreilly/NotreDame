          Calendar

          Link your calendar, get your courses.

          Domer OS reads your Google Calendar, finds the weekly events that are classes, matches each to its building and puts them on the map. It never writes to your calendar.

          1. Open Google Calendar on a computer → Settings.
          2. Under Settings for my calendars, pick your nd.edu calendar.
          3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format.
